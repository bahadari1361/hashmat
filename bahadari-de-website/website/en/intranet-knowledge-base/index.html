Intranet and knowledge base on SharePoint

A SharePoint intranet where field staff and office find what they need – organised by role, not by folder.

Knowledge lives in people’s heads, in folders and in chat threads. New staff ask the same questions, and forms exist in three versions. A well-structured intranet gives each role its own entry point and turns requests into digital processes.

What I take care of

  • Role-based concept: field service, office, management
  • SharePoint pages for procedures, safety, first aid, contacts and training
  • Digital requests with Microsoft Forms and an approval workflow
  • Embedded Power Apps, for example for meeting actions or customer requests
  • Onboarding material and standard operating procedures, bilingual if needed
  • Consistent look with custom graphics

What you get

  • One entry point instead of scattered documents
  • Faster onboarding for new staff
  • Requests with a traceable approval history

Tools & platforms

  • SharePoint Online
  • Power Apps
  • Power Automate
  • Microsoft Forms
  • Microsoft Teams
