This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and embeds no third-party content. Fonts are served locally from this site, not from Google.

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time of access, the requested page and browser information to deliver the site and keep it secure. If you email me, I process your details to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Data is deleted once it’s no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

More: Microsoft privacy statement.
