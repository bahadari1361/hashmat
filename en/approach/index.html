Approach

Clear phases, visible results and decisions that stay with you. That keeps every project manageable.

  1. AnalysisJoint review of the current state: systems, processes, weak points and goals. Result: a short written assessment.
  2. DesignSolution outline with diagram, effort estimate and alternatives. Result: a proposal for you to decide on.
  3. PilotDelivery on a small scale with real data and a few users. Result: a tested solution and feedback.
  4. RolloutIntroduction for everyone involved with a short training. Result: the solution in production.
  5. OperateDocumentation, handover and ongoing support if you wish. Result: a solution your team understands.

Principles

  • Security and data protection are part of the solution from day one.
  • I build on existing licences and systems.
  • Every solution is documented with diagrams and guides.
  • Process models in BPMN, architectures in standard notation.
