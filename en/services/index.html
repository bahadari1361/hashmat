Frequently asked questions

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. Most Microsoft 365 and Power Platform work happens remotely. For networking, hardware and training I come on site around Bremen.

How is work billed?

By time spent or as a fixed price for clearly scoped projects. We agree the framework in writing after the intro call.

Do we need new licences?

Often not. I build on what you already license first, and tell you honestly when an upgrade is really needed.

What happens to our data?

I work with only the permissions a task needs and document every access. Confidentiality can be agreed in writing.

Can you take over existing solutions?

Yes. I analyse existing flows, apps or tenant settings, document them and develop them further.

Is there a task that eats up your time every week?

In a free 30-minute introductory call we look at what can be simplified or automated.
