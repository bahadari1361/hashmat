Frequently asked questions

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. Microsoft 365 and automation mostly remotely; network, hardware and training also on site around Bremen.

How is work billed?

By time spent or as a fixed price for clearly scoped projects. We agree the framework in writing after the first call.

Do we need new licences?

Often not. I build on what you have and only recommend upgrades when they are really needed.

How do you handle our data?

With least privilege, documented access and, if you wish, a written confidentiality agreement.

Can you take over existing solutions?

Yes. I analyse existing systems, flows and settings, document them and develop them further.
