Intranet & Documentation

Intranet, knowledge base and IT documentation

An intranet where every role finds what it needs – and documentation that holds up when it matters.

Knowledge often lives in people’s heads, folders and chat threads. A well-structured intranet gives each role its own entry point, and clean IT documentation keeps operations running without depending on individuals.

What I take care of

  • Intranet concept organised by role, not by folder
  • SharePoint hub with portals, news and quick links
  • Digital reports and requests with approval
  • Standard operating procedures and onboarding material, bilingual if needed
  • IT operations manual: network diagram, inventory, access and emergency concepts
  • Training material and short introductions

Your outcome

  • One entry point instead of scattered files
  • Faster onboarding
  • Documentation that stands up to audits

Technologies

  • Power Apps
  • Microsoft Forms
  • Power Automate
  • Teams
