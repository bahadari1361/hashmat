System Administration

System administration, networking and IT security

Reliable operation of network, Wi-Fi, firewall, servers and workstations – secure and documented.

Good IT goes unnoticed: sign-in, printers, Wi-Fi and backups simply work. That takes a clean network design, clear permissions and regular care. I take on these operations and bring experience from a large, security-critical environment in the financial sector.

What I take care of

  • Network design with VLAN segmentation for workstations, servers, Wi-Fi and guests
  • Firewall configuration with clear zones and rules, VPN with two-factor sign-in
  • Wi-Fi planning: internal network with 802.1X, isolated guest network
  • User and permission management, onboarding and offboarding
  • Servers, clients, updates and patch management
  • Backup following the 3-2-1 rule with regular restore tests

Your outcome

  • A securely segmented, well-structured network
  • Fewer disruptions day to day
  • Complete documentation for emergencies and cover
