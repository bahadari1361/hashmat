Automation

Digital requests and approvals

From paper form to digital request with approval in Teams and a complete history.

Typical situation

Requests – for materials, workwear or leave – run on paper or by email. Status is unclear and follow-ups cost time.

Solution

  1. FormA short form, reachable from the intranet, Teams or a phone.
  2. ApprovalPower Automate starts the approval; managers decide directly in Teams or Outlook.
  3. FulfilmentApproved requests go to administration, rejections go back with a reason.
  4. RecordEach request is stored in a list with decision and timestamp.

Benefits

  • Status visible at all times
  • No lost paperwork
  • Traceable approval history

Technical detail

Forms trigger, “Start and wait for an approval” action, branching on outcome, storage in a SharePoint list.
