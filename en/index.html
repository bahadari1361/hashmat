How I work

No thick concept paper up front, but small steps that quickly show whether something works.

  1. UnderstandI look at how the process really runs today – not how it’s written in the manual.
  2. PilotA first version with a small team and real data. Whatever doesn’t hold up gets dropped.
  3. Roll outOnly once the pilot convinces does the solution go to everyone – with a short introduction.
  4. Hand overYou get documentation that lets your team maintain the solution themselves.
