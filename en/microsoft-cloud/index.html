Microsoft 365 and Azure – set up properly and secured

Setting up, securing and structuring Microsoft 365 and Azure so your team actually enjoys working with it.

Many companies already pay for Microsoft 365 but use a fraction of it. Teams and SharePoint often lack a clear structure, and security settings are still on default. I bring order to the tenant and secure it in a way that fits the size of your business.

What I take care of

  • Setting up and administering Microsoft 365 tenants
  • Identity and access with Entra ID: users, groups, MFA, Conditional Access policies
  • Structure for Teams and SharePoint: who sees what, where documents live
  • Exchange Online: mailboxes, shared mailboxes, reviewing forwarding rules
  • Tenant security reviews: sign-in logs, suspicious accounts, external sharing
  • Azure fundamentals and resource hardening based on AZ-500 practice

What you get

  • A tenant whose structure you understand
  • A noticeably smaller attack surface for phishing and account takeover
  • Clear ownership of files, teams and permissions
