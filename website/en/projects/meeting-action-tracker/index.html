Action tracker for the weekly management meeting

A Power App for management that tracks actions from the weekly meeting with owners, status and deadlines.

Starting point

Actions from the weekly meeting were minuted but not followed up. Whether something was done often only became clear at the next meeting.

Approach

  1. Data modelA SharePoint list with a German column schema as the base, shaped around how the meeting actually runs.
  2. InterfacePower Apps instead of Microsoft Lists or Power BI, for a clear interface with KPI tiles and colour-coded status.
  3. NotificationsPower Automate informs owners about new and overdue actions.

Outcome

  • Open items are visible before every meeting
  • Every action has an owner and a status

Technical details

German umlauts in internal column names get encoded (ä → _x00e4_). Multi-person fields caused JSON errors and were split into single- and multi-person columns; data loads via ClearCollect.
