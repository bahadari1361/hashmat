Role-based intranet and knowledge base

A SharePoint intranet with separate portals for field service, office and management – including digital requests and embedded apps.

Starting point

Management wanted one place where field technicians and office staff could find everything. Instructions, forms and contacts were scattered, and new employees depended on asking around.

Approach

  1. Roles, not foldersInstead of a folder tree there are three portals: technicians (customer service, requests, safety & regulations, organisation), office and management.
  2. Build content pagesPages for first aid, contacts, training and a guide for handling customer complaints.
  3. Digitise requestsWorkwear orders go through Microsoft Forms with an approval workflow in Power Automate.
  4. Embed appsPower Apps replace lists where a better interface is needed, such as the meeting tracker.
  5. Support onboardingA bilingual onboarding procedure for field technicians and a script for a safety training video.

Outcome

  • One entry point for every employee
  • Requests with a traceable approval instead of paper and hallway conversations
  • Consistent look with custom graphics and a personalised start page

Technical details

The mega menu wasn’t available due to hub-site registration restrictions in the tenant, so navigation was solved through page structure and tiles. An HTML contact directory wouldn’t render inside SharePoint and was rebuilt as a Power App.
